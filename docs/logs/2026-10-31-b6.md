## Incident: GND jumper slipped off the ESP32 (possible short)

While the board was powered with `bench` loaded, the tester reported that the GND jumper on the ESP32 slipped and a short
may have occurred ("no sé si hice corto"); the OLED looked dark. What the pin touched was not recorded.

Findings afterwards:
- The OLED being dark with `bench` is normal (that firmware never draws on the display).
- The USB-serial bridge still enumerated (COM5) and the tester reported nothing hot and no smell.
- After reflashing the real firmware (BOOT had to be released and EN pressed before it ran), serial printed
  `display: ok, storage: ok, tx: ok, reset: encendido` and the tester saw the menu on the OLED.
- Conclusion: the ESP32, the OLED and its I2C wiring survived. Not checked: the MOSFET and the protoboard resistors (they
  passed the diode-mode test before the incident; re-test the MOSFET before relying on it).
- Also noted: `storage: ok` now (it failed at the first boot). The flash was probably formatted from the UI in between;
  not confirmed by the tester.
- Lesson: use the breadboard rails for GND instead of single jumpers on the board's pins, and fix jumpers with the board
  unpowered.
