# 2026-11-02 — Battery power bring-up (B9, part 1)

Hardware: 18650 in a holder, TP4056-class USB-C charger module with protection (read from the photo; chip markings
not independently confirmed), XL6009 boost module. Cell wires were held against the pads by hand (not soldered), so
this is a bench test only.

## Flash of the full firmware
- `esp32dev_ssd1306` built (flash 53.6 %, RAM 7.3 %) and uploaded with BOOT held; hash verified.
- Observed by the user: the icon-grid menu renders well on the real panel.
  Not yet checked on the panel: keyboard, suggested names, import screen, accents/ñ/¡¿.

## Measurements (multimeter Noga DT-838)
| Point | Reading | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cell alone | 3.82 V | healthy, about half charged |
| Charger `OUT+`/`OUT-` with cell on `B+`/`B-` | 3.82 V | protection passes the cell voltage |
| XL6009 input | 3.87 V | |
| XL6009 output, first reading | 0 V (display "1" = over range in 20 V) | trimmer was set high; > 20 V |
| XL6009 output after trimmer | 5.00 V unloaded | set with the ESP32 disconnected |
| `VIN`-`GND` with the board running on battery | 4.96 V | OLED on, menu works; USB unplugged |

Temperature (by touch): XL6009 and charger cold.

## Result
- The XL6009 starts and holds ~5 V from a single cell at 3.87 V under the board's load. Not tested yet: lower cell
  voltage (it may stop sooner than the cell's cut-off), the current peak during IR transmission, a long run.
- The 0 V first seen was the trimmer set above the multimeter range, not a dead module.

## Emitter driver replaced by an NPN (2N2222, plastic TO-92), 2026-11-03
- Pin order found by the user: from the flat face, left to right E, B, C (from the curved face C, B, E).
- Circuit on the breadboard: GPIO23 -> 1 kΩ -> base; emitter -> GND; collector -> LED cathode; LED anode -> 220 Ω -> VIN (5 V).
  The 100 kΩ base pull-down was NOT fitted for this test (to be fitted on the final board).
- Idle: 0.01 V across the 220 Ω resistor (transistor off, LED not conducting).
- Sending a button of an imported IRDB remote (Importar from the SD card): the user saw the LED blink on the phone's
  front camera and "it works excellently". Range and LED current while sending were not measured.
- The "no direct GPIO -> LED" rule is satisfied again; the temporary exception in `docs/wiring.md` is closed.
